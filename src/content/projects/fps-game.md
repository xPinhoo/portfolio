## Why

I've played first-person shooters on PC for as long as I can remember. After years of wondering how they work under the hood, the natural next step was to build one myself.

## Where it stands

Early days. I'm following a structured learning plan for game development in **Unreal Engine** and building the foundations one piece at a time: movement, shooting, and the systems that make a shooter feel good to play.

## What I'm learning

Game development is a different world from the web: real-time loops, physics, performance budgets measured in milliseconds per frame. It's the same curiosity that got me into code, pointed at something new.

_This page will grow as the game does._
