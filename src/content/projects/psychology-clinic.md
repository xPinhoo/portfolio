## The context

A company building the website for a psychology clinic got stuck: the site needed to do more than a standard template allowed, and they asked me to step in and help.

## What I did

- Worked out the **logic behind online appointment scheduling**, so patients can book directly from the site.
- Helped structure the pages that present the **clinic's professionals**, their areas of work and the **clinic's vision**.
- Bent WordPress to the clinic's requirements rather than the other way around.

## What I took from it

Jumping into someone else's project, understanding it quickly and unblocking it is a skill in itself, and it's much of what day-to-day front-end work in a team looks like.
